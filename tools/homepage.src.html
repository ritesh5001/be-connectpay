How quickly will I receive my money?
With next-day settlement, card payments you take today are paid into your business bank account on the next working day.
Which payment methods can I accept?
All major credit and debit cards including Visa, Mastercard and American Express, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay, by chip & PIN or contactless.
Do I need Wi-Fi to use a card machine?
Not with a mobile terminal. It has a built-in SIM card and connects anywhere with 4G signal. Countertop and portable machines use your broadband or Wi-Fi.
Can I take payments online or over the phone?
Yes. Our payment gateway integrates with most shopping carts, and Pay by Link lets you send a secure payment request by email or text for bookings, deposits and phone orders.
Will I get help setting up?
Yes. Your terminal is easy to set up, and our UK-based support team is available 24/7 to walk you through it or help with anything afterwards.

Complete the quick quote form at the top of this page or call us on 020 7052 7978. A payments specialist will recommend the right setup and send you a clear quote.
