How quickly will I receive my money?

With next-day settlement, card payments you take today are paid into your business bank account on the next working day.

Which payment methods can I accept?

All major credit and debit cards including Visa, Mastercard and American Express, plus Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay, by chip & PIN or contactless.

Do I need Wi-Fi to use a card machine?

Not with a mobile machine. It has a built-in GPRS SIM card and connects anywhere there's 4G signal. Countertop machines connect by broadband or phone line.

Can I take payments online and over the phone?

Yes. Our free payment gateway integrates with most shopping carts, and Pay by Link lets you send secure payment requests by email or text.

What support will I get?

A dedicated welcome team gets you set up, and our UK-based support is available 24/7, 7 days a week.

Can Be-Connect help with business funding?

Yes. Talk to our team about funding options based on your card takings, and we'll explain what you could access.
